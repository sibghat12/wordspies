Say the same word
as your friend.

You both type a word at the same time. You are not trying to be clever — you are trying to type the same word as each other. You won't. So then you both aim for the word sitting between the two you just said, and you keep going until your minds meet.

  • 1Round one, you both type any word at all. Reveal together.
  • 2Different words? Now you both type the word in between them.
  • 3No word twice, and you never see theirs first. Fewest rounds wins the bragging.
