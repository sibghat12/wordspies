Delete your TalkSibi account

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time. Choose whichever way is easier — both do the same thing.

1 Delete from the app (fastest)

If you can still log in, this is the quickest option:

  1. Open talksibi.com/app and log in
  2. Tap your profile (bottom-right avatar)
  3. Scroll to Settings → Delete account
  4. Confirm — that's it
Open the app

2 Delete by email (if you can't log in)

Send an email from the address you registered with so we can verify it's really you.

To: hello@talksibi.com
Subject: Delete my TalkSibi account
Body: "Please delete my account and all associated data."

Open email app

We confirm receipt within 3 working days and delete within 7 days.

3 What gets deleted

  • Your profile (name, photo, native + learning language, country, DOB)
  • Every direct message you've sent
  • Every wall post + comment
  • Voice notes + uploaded images
  • Follow / follower relationships (both directions)
  • Reactions, references, and progress data
  • Push notification tokens
  • Session cookies and login records
What we keep for 12 months — anonymous audit records of moderation actions (blocks, reports where you were reporter or reported). This is required for repeat-offender detection and doesn't identify you personally. After 12 months these are also purged.

Change your mind?

Account deletion is permanent — we can't restore data once it's gone. If you just want a break, you can sign out and take time away; your account stays inactive but recoverable for 24 months before we auto-remove it.

Under-18 accounts

TalkSibi is strictly 18+. If you're a parent or guardian and believe an under-18 has created an account, email hello@talksibi.com and we remove within 48 hours. See our Child Safety policy for full details.
