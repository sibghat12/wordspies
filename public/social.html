Our house rules

Be kind and real. WordSpies is a community of language learners around the world. Treat everyone with respect. No harassment, hate speech, spam, or nudity.

Age matters. You confirm you are 18 or over. We may ask for verification if we suspect otherwise, and we remove any account that turns out to be under 18.

Report freely. Every profile and every message has a Report and a Block button. We review reports within 24 hours and act firmly.
