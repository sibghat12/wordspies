      🏀 How to play Hoop

      You have 60 seconds to sink as many free throws as possible.

      Drag anywhere on the court to aim — a dotted line shows your throw arc. Adjust as much as you like. When you're happy, tap SHOOT. The ball follows real physics: too soft and it drops short, too hard and it clangs off the back.

      The hoop moves after every basket. Streaks earn bonus points — three in a row is worth an extra one.
