🏀 How to play Hoop

You have 60 seconds to sink as many free throws as possible.

Drag the ball back to aim — a guide line shows your throw. Release to shoot. The ball follows real physics: too soft and it drops short, too hard and it clangs off the back.

The hoop moves after every basket. Streaks earn bonus points — three in a row is worth an extra one.
