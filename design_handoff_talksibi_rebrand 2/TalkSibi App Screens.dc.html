2bMobile rules — for the handoff
How every screen adapts to mobile
📱 4 main tabs in the bottom nav — 🌍 Community, 🎲 Play, 💬 Talk, 🎓 Learn — with the ➕ Create button floating bottom-right above the nav. Sub-tabs at the top per main: Community → People | Live · Play → Games | Parties · Talk → Chats | AI Experts · Learn → My plan | IELTS & TOEFL. Me lives on the avatar in the top bar. Desktop keeps the full top-tab row.
🃏 Member cards go from 3-column grid to single-column rows (photo left, one CTA right). Hit targets ≥ 44px.
💬 Chat is full-screen with a back arrow; the chat list is its own screen (already how social.html works).
🎉 Party room (1c), login (1e) and profile (1f) are designed at 380-390px — they ARE the mobile layout.
🖥 Landing page: hero stacks (copy above, chat illustration below), games grid 3→2 columns, footer columns stack, full-bleed CTAs keep 20px side padding.
👆 Filter chips scroll horizontally, never wrap past one row. Sticky elements: top bar + bottom nav only.
